Does shell crossing leave an irreducible k² long-mode response? — paired-IC 1–7LPT vs collisionless PM

Rendered with all figures: https://yipihey.github.io/DiscoDJ.jl/studies/eft_shell_crossing/.

Study of the "irreducible effective stress" claim of Carrasco, Hertzberg & Senatore (2012, arXiv:1206.2926) with DiscoDJ.jl (DiscoDJNative, GPU).

Result in one paragraph. Two 3D universes share their long modes bitwise and differ by a band of short modes (0.2–1.2 h Mpc⁻¹); the long-mode displacement response Δψ_L = −C₂k²ψ_L is measured at the field level, with 1–7th-order LPT (the pressureless single-stream fluid to increasing accuracy) and with a discreteness-suppressed collisionless PM validated to reproduce 7LPT to 10⁻⁴ before shell crossing. Before crossing the single-stream series converges onto PM (PM − 7LPT ≤ 2×10⁻⁵ against an 8 % 3LPT→7LPT change). Immediately after first crossing a coherent, pure-k², pressure-signed residual appears that is common to 3, 5, 7LPT and to the extrapolated series limit, while that series is still convergent (0.225 < D < 0.5); it grows with the shell-crossed mass (∝ f^1.5), moves with the crossing time when the band amplitude changes, is absent in redshift-space-only crossing, is resolution-converged, and is accounted for — in sign and to a factor ≈ 2 in magnitude — by the velocity-dispersion stress measured directly in PM's multistream regions. Varying the cutoff, the collisionless response runs like the CHS (2012) one-loop running while 4LPT/7LPT fail once the integrated-out modes have crossed; it is carried by modes near the nonlinear scale, deeper modes decouple (for the random long field; not at ε → 0, §14.11), and it is a coherent effect (at k = 0.06 h Mpc⁻¹, modes below 5 h⁻¹Mpc change P(k) by −2.1 % coherently and add 0.05 % stochastic power). Assessment: strong support (category 1) for the CHS irreducibility statement within the window where it can be tested (§12); beyond D ≈ 0.5 the single-stream series diverges and irreducibility is an extrapolation.

Follow-up results and final reports (§16): synthesis; A: density response; B: two-loop stress; C: σ(M), LPT and spherical collapse; D: reach of the UV; E: campaign notes.

Precision follow-up with plane-wave long modes (§14). Replacing the random long field by ±ε plane waves isolates the coefficient of the linear long field. In the displacement, where ε = 0.01 is already the ε → 0 limit, it is measured to 0.4–5 % at 7 cutoffs (k_split = 0.12–0.7) and 12 epochs from 8 PM runs per cutoff. Findings: * the departure from the converged pressureless series is significant from first crossing at every cutoff; * at D = 1 the displacement coefficient is small and negative (−0.40 at k_split = 0.2), carried by modes near k_NL. The Eulerian density, which is what P(k) sees, is instead suppressed: C₂,δ = +1.94 ± 0.02, ΔP/P = −0.97 % at k = 0.05, and it matches one-loop PT to ≤ 1.2 % before crossing (§14.11; an earlier version of this page wrongly quoted +0.2 % in P). In the displacement, modes beyond ≈ 0.35 h Mpc⁻¹ have decoupled and the shells' effects do not add; in the density, modes above 0.33 still carry a third of the response at D = 1; * about half of the §6 coefficient (+1.51) is long × short loops (+2.06; not ∝ a² after D ≈ 0.6); * at D ≳ 0.5, "PM − 7LPT" mostly measures the divergence of the LPT series; the counterterm at fixed loop order changes sign in time; * a positive k⁴ departure is detected at k_split ≥ 0.5; * the second-order kernel acquires a suppressive counterterm (−0.54 ± 0.13 of tree at D = 1); * no coherent second-order vorticity (< 1 % of tree).

These map one-to-one onto the two-loop EFT counterterms of Anastasiou+ (2025, arXiv:2509.05187), §14.9.

0. The question, and what would count as evidence

After coarse-graining, unresolved short-scale dynamics act on long modes through an effective stress τ ⊃ c_s² δ_L, i.e. a long-mode displacement response

Δψ_L(k) = −C₂(a) k² ψ_L(k) + O(k⁴) + ε(k).

The strong claim tested here: the finite renormalized coefficient is an irreducible error of the pressureless single-stream fluid — making the perturbative pressureless solution more accurate does not remove the part generated by multistreaming once shell crossing occurs.

We do not try to fit an EFT to a power spectrum. We construct two universes that share their long modes bitwise and differ only by a band of short modes, evolve both with (i) the pressureless single-stream solution to increasing order (1–7LPT) and (ii) the exact collisionless dynamics (PM N-body), and measure the field-level response of the shared long modes. The hypothesis makes falsifiable predictions:

prediction if the hypothesis is right what would falsify it
P1 before any shell crossing, PM − LPT_n → 0 as n grows a pre-crossing PM−LPT residual that does not converge (→ numerics or non-perturbative physics)
P2 after crossing, PM − LPT_n tends to a nonzero limit as n grows, i.e. PM departs from the limit of the single-stream series PM − LPT_n keeps decreasing with n after crossing
P3 the departure has the EFT form −C₂k² (a k_max plateau), is deterministic (correlated with ψ_L) and grows with the shell-crossed mass departure not ∝ k², or uncorrelated with ψ_L (stochastic), or unrelated to crossing
P4 stochastic UV→IR leakage is suppressed at low k (P_ε,δ ∝ k⁴) and is separable from the deterministic part
P5 (EFT) the response is dominated by modes near the nonlinear scale; very deep UV modes decouple response grows with the deepest UV modes

2. Experimental design

Background. Einstein–de Sitter (Ω_m = 1, D₊ = a) with the Planck18 linear spectrum shape (σ₈ = 0.8105 at D = 1). In EdS the general-order nLPT of compute_core (Ψ = Σ Dⁿψₙ) is the exact fastest-growing single-stream solution, and PM integrates the same equations — so "finite LPT order" is the only difference between the single-stream curves, and there is no growth-approximation confound between LPT and PM. Time is labelled by D = a.

Paired initial conditions (src/ics.jl). One real-space unit white-noise field per seed on a fixed 256³ grid; potential φ(k) = ω̂(k)√(P(k)(N/L)³)(−1/k²) (DiscoDJNative faithful convention, ψ₁ = −∇φ):

ω̂_IR = s_L·1[|k| < k_split] ω̂_L, ω̂_UV = ω̂_IR + A·1[k_lo ≤ |k| < k_uv] ω̂_S.

Every mode with |k| < k_split is bitwise identical in the two members (tested), so there is no initial k² difference — unlike Gaussian-filter pairs (§5). Fiducial: L = 500 h⁻¹Mpc, k_split = 0.2, k_uv = 1.2 h Mpc⁻¹ (band σ_δ = 1.91 D, σ_ψ = 4.0 D h⁻¹Mpc; long modes σ_δ = 0.71 D). The response is measured at k ≤ 0.08 ≪ k_split (6 fundamental shells; 0.0126 ≤ k ≤ 0.19 stored). Realizations are embedded exactly (mode by mode) into finer particle grids, so LPT on 256³ and PM on 384³/512³ evolve the same universe.

Ensemble structure (config/*.toml, [seeds]). For each long seed L and short seed S both long-mode signs s_L = ±1 are run:

  • the odd-in-L combination ½[Δψ(+L,S) − Δψ(−L,S)] removes the L-independent short×short term exactly — it isolates the deterministic response;
  • the even-in-L part ½[Δψ(+L,S) + Δψ(−L,S)] carries the stochastic leakage;
  • several short seeds at fixed L give the conditional mean ⟨Δψ|L⟩ and the conditional variance Var_{S|L}(Δψ) directly (Experiment B).

Errors are delete-one jackknife over long seeds (the independent unit).

Observables (src/measure.jl, all on the GPU, reduced to the low-k Fourier modes on the device): Lagrangian displacement ψ(q) = x − q; velocity Π = dΨ/dD; CIC density (window-deconvolved); the fraction of the 6N³ equal-mass Kuhn tetrahedra whose orientation is inverted (shell-crossed mass).

Estimators (src/estimators.jl, scripts/analyze.jl). With x = ψ_L∥ = D k̂·ψ̂₁ (the exact linear displacement, identical in both members):

R(k) = Σ Re(Δψ_∥ x*) / Σ|x|² (binned), R = −C₂k² [+ C₄k⁴] fitted for k ≤ k_max (12 cutoffs);

the same with the Eulerian density (regressor δ_L = −i|k|x) and with the velocity (regressor ψ₁∥). The PM − LPT_n residual is computed realization by realization (Δψ_PM − Δψ_LPTn regressed on x), which cancels the common short×short noise; the single-stream limit LPT_∞ is estimated from the odd partial sums (3, 5, 7) by a Shanks/geometric-tail transform where the increment ratio is < 0.7.

Dimensional dictionary. C₂ is a comoving length². A stress term −c_s²(k/a)²δ in the EdS growth equation, δ'' + (3/2a)δ' − (3/2a²)δ = −k² ℓ²(a) δ/a² with ℓ² ≡ c_s²/(aH)², has for ℓ² ∝ a^m the particular solution Δδ/δ_L = −k² ℓ²/[m(m+5/2)]. Hence

ℓ²(a) = m(m + 5/2) C₂(a), c_s²(a) = (aH)² ℓ²(a), m = d ln C₂/d ln D,

valid only where C₂ is a power law. For the density, ΔP/P_L = 2R = −2C₂k² (the P_UV = P_IR − 2c_s²k²P_L convention of the PreCrossedSheet Lab and of CHS up to their k_NL normalisation). We do not identify our band-response C₂ with the CHS c_s² of the full ΛCDM field: it is the response to one added band, and the mapping above assumes time-local stress.

3. Numerical methods

  • nLPT 1–7 — DiscoDJNative compute_core (general-order EdS recursion, longitudinal + transverse, 3/2-rule de-aliased), Float32 on the GPU. Grid-converged: 256³ and 384³ give the same C₂ to < 0.5 % at every order and epoch (config/conv_lpt384.toml).
  • PM — DiscoDJNative run_nbody (faithful port of DISCO-DJ, BullFrog stepper, 200 steps uniform in D from D = 0.02 with 3LPT ICs; every epoch is a step node).
  • Discreteness was the dominant numerical issue (§4): a standard CIC/finite-difference PM does not reproduce the two-loop (D⁴) part of the pre-crossing response even at 512³ particles on a 1024³ mesh. The production PM therefore uses DISCO-DJ's discreteness-suppression settings (arXiv:2309.10865): PCS assignment, window deconvolution, spectral gradient, interlacing, and 2³ Fourier sheet resampling (the displacement field is Fourier-interpolated to 8 sub-particles per particle, i.e. the density is that of the continuous Lagrangian sheet). This reproduces 7LPT to ~10⁻⁴ before crossing and is converged after it (256³ vs 384³).

DiscoDJNative changes made for this study (commits on this branch): Float32 deconvolution fix (982cc10); memory-lean PM solver PMSolverLean / run_nbody(lean=true, callback_when=…) (4e61176), which puts 512³ particles on a 1024³ mesh on the 48 GB GPU in Float32 at no accuracy cost.

4. Numerical convergence — what had to be fixed before any physics

LPT. 256³ and 384³ agree to < 0.5 % for every order and epoch (conv_lpt256 vs conv_lpt384), including orders 5–7 whose intermediate fields formally extend beyond the 256³ Nyquist frequency. The odd-order series is the object of interest: in the ensemble mean only odd orders contribute to R (the even orders' cross term with ψ_L is an odd-point function of a Gaussian field), and indeed C₂^(2n) ≈ C₂^(2n−1) (Table 1).

PM: particle discreteness, not time stepping, was the problem (fig10). A standard PM (CIC, 4th-order finite-difference force) reproduces the one-loop (3LPT) pre-crossing response but misses the two-loop D⁴ part, by an amount that depends on the particle grid:

Other checks (scripts/diagnostics.jl, results/diagnostics_fiducial.json): * PM total momentum |ΣP|/(N³ rms P) ≤ 1.8×10⁻⁴ over all runs and epochs (the spectral-gradient/interlaced force is not exactly momentum conserving; plain CIC/FD conserves it to round-off). Mass is conserved exactly. * Isotropy: C₂ from modes near the grid axes (Σk̂ᵢ⁴ > 0.7) and near the diagonals (< 0.5) agree within 1–2σ up to D = 0.6 (e.g. −0.2172 ± 0.0018 vs −0.2169 ± 0.0019 at D = 0.3); at D = 1 they differ by 4.5 % (1.609 ± 0.015 vs 1.540 ± 0.013, 3.6σ) — a modest late-time grid anisotropy, larger than the 0.7 % axis/diagonal difference of 7LPT on the same modes. * The stochastic residual of the density is ∝ k^4.0–4.2 and of the displacement ∝ k^2.0 (mass and momentum conservation).

5. Controls

  • Null pair (band amplitude 0): Δψ ≡ 0 at every LPT order (test); the PM null pair measures the GPU reproducibility floor (config/null.toml).
  • Shared long modes are bitwise identical (test), and 1LPT gives C₂ ≡ 0 at every epoch (Table 1).
  • IC artifacts (fig11, scripts/controls.jl): a PreCrossedSheet-style Gaussian pair (R = 3 / 6 h⁻¹Mpc) has a 1LPT "response" C₂ = −14.0 (analytic −13.5 = (R_UV²−R_IR²)/2) at all D — ~80× the dynamical 3LPT response at D = 0.3; "match linear variance" adds a k-independent offset that a k² fit turns into C₂ = +73. A dynamical response must vanish as D → 0; these do not.
  • Odd/even in L: with both signs of every long realization, the plain estimator is the odd estimator (x(−L) = −x(+L)), so the L-independent short × short term cancels exactly in R.
  • Estimator recovery: injected −C₂k² + C₄k⁴ responses and k² stochastic noise are recovered (tests).
  • Kinematic check: 1LPT density response = σ²_ψ,band D²/6 = 2.67 D² (measured 2.64–2.80 for D = 0.2–0.8), a pure projection effect with no dynamics.
  • Time stepping, mesh, particle number, IC order and start time — §4 and config/conv_*.

Universality with the crossed mass (fig12; variants amp07, kuv06, 2 long seeds each, sharing the fiducial IR members). Reducing the band amplitude to 0.7 delays first crossing to D ≈ 0.3 and the departure with it; a narrower band (0.2–0.6) changes the response amplitude. Plotted against the inverted mass fraction of the UV member, the fractional departure (C₂^PM − C₂^LPT)/|C₂^3LPT| of all three set-ups collapses onto one curve (< 1 % at 10⁻³ crossed mass, ≈ 10 % at 2 %, ≈ 50–80 % at 7–8 %), i.e. the departure is a function of how much mass has shell-crossed, not of D or of the band. (The variants' errors are mode-counting estimates from 2 seeds.)

The departure has the EFT form (fig7, fig5). (R_PM − R_7LPT)/k² is flat in k up to 0.19 h Mpc⁻¹ at every post-crossing epoch (prediction P3): a pure −ΔC₂k² term, with no k⁴ structure visible. The PM C₂ itself has a genuine k_max plateau (−0.089 ± 0.002 for k_max = 0.03–0.18 at D = 0.2; 1.46–1.51 at D = 1), and adding C₄k⁴ changes it by ≤ 5 %.

k⁴ convergence (scripts/c4_convergence.jl, results/c4_convergence.txt; band-edge control chs/band_edge.py). The absolute C₄ is noise-limited, not set by the proximity of the band edge: the exact one-loop response to the band [0.2, 1.2) departs from pure k² by only 1.2 / 2.6 / 5.1 % at k = 0.08 / 0.12 / 0.18, which shifts a fitted C₂ by < 0.5 % and contributes a smooth, computable C₄ ≈ +2.5 D² (Mpc/h)⁴ (density); for k_split = 0.33 and 0.5 the edge effect is 2.5 % and 1.4 % at k = 0.18. The apparent k_max dependence of the ensemble C₄ (−17.6 ± 9.5 at k_max = 0.12 vs −5.2 ± 1.1 at 0.18, D = 0.2) is statistical. Convergence is therefore judged on realization-level differences with fixed weights, where the cosmic-variance noise cancels. Before crossing the k⁴ part of the odd LPT series converges (ΔC₄ of 5−3 / 7−5 LPT: −0.022 / −0.004 at D = 0.2, −0.107 / −0.038 at 0.3), more slowly than C₂ (increment ratio 0.36 vs 0.32 at D = 0.3, 0.58 vs 0.57 at 0.4, → 1 near D = 0.5, as for C₂). The suppressed PM matches 7LPT in C₄ before crossing (ΔC₄ = 0.000–0.002 ± 0.001), whereas plain-CIC 512³ PM is off by −0.11 ± 0.017 — C₄ is a far more sensitive discreteness diagnostic than C₂. After crossing, the suppressed PM at 256³ and 384³ agree in ΔC₄ within ~1σ at every epoch (D = 0.4: 0.23 / 0.26 ± 0.05; D = 0.6: 4.29 / 4.34 ± 0.5; D = 1: 71.3 / 71.4 ± 7). The multistream residual's own k⁴ part is small (≈ 6 % of its k² part at k = 0.15, D = 0.4) but from D ≈ 0.35 it is comparable to 7LPT's k⁴ truncation, so at this split it is not separable from LPT truncation; the RG splits k_split ≥ 0.33 (k/k_split ≤ 0.55 for k ≤ 0.18) test this with the same seeds.

Experiment B — deterministic vs stochastic (fig6). With 6 short seeds at fixed long modes the conditional variance Var_{S|L}(Δ) is measured directly. At D = 1, k = 0.064 h Mpc⁻¹: coherent ΔP/P = 2R ≈ −1.6 %, stochastic P_ε/P_L ≈ 7×10⁻⁵ — the coherent response dominates by ~200×; P_ε,δ ∝ k^4.0–4.2 and P_ε,ψ ∝ k^2.0 at every epoch (mass and momentum conservation), before and after crossing (prediction P4). UV → IR coupling at these scales is a coherent modification of existing long modes, not added noise.

Density vs displacement (fig8). The Eulerian density response is dominated by the kinematic smearing of the long mode by the short displacements: 1LPT gives C₂,δ = σ²_ψ,band D²/6 = 2.67 D² with no dynamics at all; one loop (3LPT) gives 1.68 D² at D = 0.2 (SPT UV limit: (61/105)·2.67 = 1.55). PM's density coefficient stays 1.6–2.3 D² through crossing (it does not change sign), while 7LPT's density coefficient runs away (45 D² at D = 1). P(k)-ratio and density-based fits are therefore far less sensitive to the multistream physics than the Lagrangian displacement.

Velocity (fig9): the Lagrangian velocity response obeys C₂^v = d(D C₂^ψ)/dD to 1–2 % up to D = 0.4 (finite-difference limited later) — a consistency check, not new information.

Time dependence / effective sound speed. The local slope m = d ln C₂,δ/d ln D of the PM density coefficient is ≈ 2.2–2.4 for 0.3 ≤ D ≤ 0.6 and falls to 1.4 by D = 1; the displacement coefficient changes sign. 7.1 What CHS's Λ is, and how it maps onto a sharp split

CHS smooth the fields with a Gaussian W_Λ(k) = exp(−k²/2Λ²) (their Eq. 14; real-space width 1/Λ, so Λ = 1/3 h Mpc⁻¹ ↔ σ_x = 3 h⁻¹Mpc), and the loops use P·exp(−k²/Λ²) (Eq. 49). Their running (Eq. 62, k_ext = 0.01) is c²(Λ) = c²(∞) + lim_{k→0}[P13(k,∞) − P13(k,Λ)] / (−2k² Γ P), with the counterterm Green's factor of their Eq. 57 / App. C Eq. 83, R_ct = −c² k²/(9ℋ₀²f₀²). In the k_ext → 0 limit P13 → −(61/105)k²σ_v²P, so the running depends on Λ only through the removed velocity variance Δσ_v² = (1/6π²)∫P(1 − e^{−q²/Λ²})dq. A sharp split k_s is therefore equivalent to the Λ that removes the same σ_v²: Λ = 1/3 ↔ k_s = 0.21, Λ = 1/6 ↔ 0.115, Λ = 0.2 (a 5 h⁻¹Mpc Gaussian) ↔ k_s = 0.136 (chs/chs_running.json, nearly identical for their and our spectrum).

Units. Our density coefficient is C₂,δ = c²/(9ℋ₀²f₀²). In our EdS run (f = 1, a = 1), c²[10⁻⁶ c²] = C₂,δ[(h⁻¹Mpc)²] exactly; in the CHS cosmology (Ω_m = 0.25, f₀ ≈ 0.48) the same c² is a 4.4× larger response. At fixed σ₈ the response C₂ (a length²) is the nearly cosmology-independent quantity; the c² normalisation carries f₀². Both are shown (figR1, figR1 in c² units).

10. Experiment 2 — is the departure the multistream kinetic stress?

Measurement (config/stress.toml, scripts/stress_analysis.jl; 4 long seeds × ±L, PM and 7LPT). At every snapshot each member deposits (NGP) mass, momentum and kinetic moments with u = dx/d ln a on 64³ and 128³ cells (7.8 and 3.9 h⁻¹Mpc) and forms the velocity-dispersion stress with the smooth single-stream flow removed per cell (least-squares linear velocity field: τ = n[Cov(u) − Cov(u,x)Cov(x)⁻¹Cov(x,u)]; this suppresses the finite-cell "dispersion" of a smooth flow 30–125× in tests). Cells containing particles of an inverted Lagrangian tetrahedron are flagged as multistream; flagged cells with an unflagged neighbour are "boundary" (the outermost caustic / splashback shell at that resolution). The longitudinal long-mode stress response c²_τ(D) = Re⟨k̂k̂:Δτ̂ δ_L*⟩/(ρ̄⟨|δ_L|²⟩) drives the long displacement through the EdS Green's function y'' + y'/2 − (3/2)y = c²_τ a (′ = d/d ln a, C₂,σ = y/a), giving the response the stress implies.

Validation. Before crossing the stress response of PM and 7LPT agrees to 3–4 digits (0.3217 vs 0.3217 at D = 0.2, 7.8 Mpc/h cells): the residual coarse-graining artifacts are identical and cancel. After crossing 7LPT's coarse-grained "stress" explodes (×140 PM's at D = 1) because its crossed streams fly apart ballistically — but LPT's equations of motion contain no stress, so this is a diagnostic of unphysical streaming, not a force. The test is therefore whether PM's own multistream stress accounts for PM's departure from the single-stream series.

13. Final scientific assessment

Category 1 — strong support, within a stated window. A converged, deterministic, pure-k² long-mode response remains relative to increasingly accurate single-stream dynamics, and it correlates with — and is quantitatively attributable to — multistream evolution: 1. Convergence before crossing, not after. PM − LPT_n falls ~10× per odd order before first crossing and becomes a common, order-independent residual after it, while the single-stream series itself still converges (D < 0.5). 2. Tied to real-space shell crossing. Onset coincides with the first inverted tetrahedra (D ≈ 0.225); the residual scales as (crossed mass)^1.5; reducing the band amplitude delays both crossing and departure, and the fractional departure of all band variants collapses onto one function of the crossed mass; redshift-space crossing (D ≈ 0.13), a pure projection, produces no departure. 3. EFT form. (R_PM − R_7LPT)/k² is flat to k = 0.19 h Mpc⁻¹; C₂ has a k_max plateau; the k⁴ remainder is ~6 % at k = 0.15 and resolution-converged; real-space spheres obey Δδ_R = C₂∇²δ_R with the predicted R-dependence. 4. Physical origin. PM's own multistream velocity-dispersion stress reproduces the departure's sign and magnitude to a factor ≈ 2 (coarse-graining dependent), living first in the caustic/splashback layers and then in the interiors. 5. Running. With the cutoff, PM runs like one-loop/CHS while 4LPT and 7LPT fail; the response is carried by modes near k_NL and deeper modes decouple (random long field; not at ε → 0, §14.11).

What the data do not establish. (i) "Irreducible" is demonstrated relative to the 7th-order single-stream solution and its extrapolated limit only for D ≲ 0.5; beyond, the series diverges and the statement is an extrapolation. (ii) The stress budget is the kinetic part only, coarse-graining dependent by a factor ~2; resolved Reynolds and gravitational stresses were not measured. (iii) Absolute agreement with the CHS coefficient is only established to a factor ≈ 3 (EdS vs ΛCDM, band-limited UV member, c² vs response normalisation); the running shape is the robust comparison. (iv) One box size (500 h⁻¹Mpc), one spectrum shape, EdS background.

Revision after the plane-wave follow-up (§14). Points 1–2 hold and are sharpened: against the converged series limit the departure is significant from D = 0.3 at all seven cutoffs. Point 4 and the D = 1 numbers of §6/§7 describe the random long field, which includes long × short loops (about half of the departure, and the sign of the D = 1 coefficient). The linear-long-field coefficient at k_split = 0.2, D = 1 is −0.40 ± 0.01 in the displacement (Table 3) and +1.94 ± 0.02 in the density (Table 6), each with its own running. At D ≳ 0.5 the irreducibility statement is about PM versus a divergent pressureless series, and the fixed-loop-order counterterm changes sign in time (Table 4).

Unexpected findings worth following up. The pressureless response is negative (short modes enhance the long displacement) and the multistream contribution reverses it; in density, multistreaming reduces the diffusive mass flux (trapping) while adding pressure, so density-based EFT fits see a much smaller effect than the displacement; plain PM silently misses the two-loop response through particle discreteness, and C₄ is the most sensitive diagnostic of that; LPT converges onto PM in redshift space through redshift-space crossing.

14. Precision counterterms with plane-wave long modes (ε → 0)

§6–§13 used random long modes (all |k| < k_split at full ΛCDM amplitude). That answers "how do the realistic long modes respond", but it mixes two things the EFT keeps apart: the linear counterterm (the coefficient of the linear long field, CHS's c_s) and terms in which the long modes themselves run in the loop. It is also noisy. A noise budget of the fiducial ensemble showed why: at fixed long modes, changing only the short seed scatters the per-pair C₂ by as much as changing the long seed (PM, D = 1: 0.022 vs 0.042). Pairs of short modes couple different long modes into each measured mode, and the ±L pairing cannot cancel that. Here the long field is replaced by a few small-amplitude plane waves.

14.1 Design

  • Long field = s ε Σ_j cos(k_j·x) (δ amplitude ε at D = 1, sign s = ±1), 12 waves per run, |k_j| log-spaced from 0.028 to min(0.6 k_split, 0.3) h Mpc⁻¹ in random directions. No wave equals ± another, and no k_a ± k_b lands on a third wave (config/prod_ks*.toml, PairSpec(pw=…, pw_amp=…)). The band is the study's: modes k_split ≤ |k| < 1.2 of each realization's own white noise (single field, 4 realizations).
  • Odd combination. Δ_j = [ψ(+ε) − ψ(−ε)]/2 − x_j at k_j, with x_j = D ψ₁(k_j). The band-free member of a plane wave is exactly Zel'dovich: no self-coupling and no crossing at ε = 0.01. Then R_j = Re(Δ_j x_j*)/|x_j|² and R(k) = −C₂k² + C₄k⁴. Every term even in ε cancels exactly, including the band's own field.
  • Pilot (config/pw_*.toml, scripts/pw_response.jl):
  • ε = 0.01 and 0.02 give the same displacement departure to ≤ 0.5 %, so ε = 0.01 is already the ε → 0 limit there. The density is different: at finite ε the waves smear one another, which is removed in §14.11.
  • The noise is independent of ε, so PM chaos sets no floor.
  • 6 waves in one run reproduce a single-wave run to 2 % at D = 1 and better earlier.
  • Long background (prod_ks20_bg05/10, PairSpec(pw_bg=a)): the same waves on a fixed random long field (|k| < k_split) scaled by a = 0.5 or 1. The background is not flipped with s, and its own modes at ±k_j are removed. The odd combination is then the response of a linear wave in a nonlinear long background. These configs run the band-free member (background + waves) too.
  • A/B sign groups (prod_ks20_ab, PairSpec(pw_flip=…)): waves are split into groups A and B (alternate in |k|) and the signs (+,+), (−,−), (+,−), (−,+) are run. [ψ(++) − ψ(+−) − ψ(−+) + ψ(−−)]/4 at k_a ± k_b isolates the second-order A×B coupling exactly.
  • Every PM run has a 1–7LPT twin on identical ICs (seconds each), so PM − LPT_n is realization-matched.

16. Follow-ups: two-loop stress, σ(M) and the reach of the UV

Full accounts are in reports/, and the two-loop design is in twoloop/README.md. These runs are EdS with the Planck18 shape, 4 seeds × ±L at fixed amplitudes, with spectra k < 1.6, 1.0, 0.6 and a UV-divergent one (P ∝ k⁻¹ above k = 0.3), plus IR members cut at Λ = 0.1–0.3. Errors exclude cosmic variance.

Two-loop stress (report B, figT1, figT2, figT3).

  • In far-UV bands at D = 0.1, PM's stress c̄/I is 0.86–0.87 (k = 0.6–1.6) and 0.88–0.89 (k = 1.0–1.6), against the analytic 61/70 = 0.871, and 1.04–1.05 in the divergent spectrum's k = 1.0–1.6 band, where 7LPT agrees. dc̄/dI across Λ agrees in all spectra only because those modes are shared; it is not a UV test.
  • Through the EdS Green's function, the measured c(D) gives density and momentum to ≤ 4.4 % at Λ = 0.15 to D = 0.5 in Planck-shape spectra (≤ 8.5 % at Λ = 0.1), and misses by 13–17 % from D ≈ 0.3 in the divergent spectrum with k < 1.6 (3–11 % with k < 1.0).
  • The second- and third-order coefficients are not determined by this estimator, so whether all eight counterterms are needed remains undecided.

σ(M), LPT and spherical collapse (report C, figM1, figM2, figM3, figM4, figM6, figM7, figM8).

  • σ(ln V) around points drawn from the density, the data-centred kNN-CDF, departs earliest from linear: +24 % at M = 10¹⁶ h⁻¹M☉, D = 1, against +7 % for Lagrangian patches and +0.5 % for fixed-radius spheres. M uses Ω_m = 1.
  • LPT converges to ≤ 0.3 % at D = 0.3 and R ≥ 5 h⁻¹Mpc from 3LPT up, then stops improving with order. By D = 0.8 5LPT and 7LPT are worse than Zel'dovich. At D = 1 no order recovers fixed-radius σ to 5 % below R ≈ 18.
  • Exact spherical collapse with density-dependent contraction reaches σ(δ_R) to 5 % down to R ≈ 6 at D = 1 (R ≈ 11 in the divergent spectrum, and R ≈ 35 without contraction). Patches fail at R₀ ≈ 9–11 and random centres at R₀ ≈ 19. At 1 % it is no better than 3LPT at D = 1 (both R ≈ 50). It also reproduces the non-Gaussian ln V distribution over 2–3 decades of probability at M ≳ 10¹⁵.
  • Patch images become elongated (a₃/a₁ = 0.47 at 1.3 × 10¹⁵, D = 1), yet bins in δ_L account for 86–97 % of Var(ln V) at M ≥ 1.3 × 10¹⁵.

UV reach (report D, figU1). Modes k = 0.6–1.6 change the variance by −0.58 % at R = 20 and −0.07 % at R = 78 h⁻¹Mpc at D = 1, after removing their added linear power. The fractional change flattens by D ≈ 0.8, and the deepest divergent band turns over. Spherical collapse misses this response.

Scales (figM5). Equating Δ²(k, D) with σ²(R, D) gives kR = 1.37–1.46 at R = 6–60 h⁻¹Mpc for a smoothed spectrum, nearly independent of D, with BAO adding −0.04 to +0.05. So M ≈ 3.6 × 10¹² k⁻³ h⁻¹M☉ for Ω_m = 1.
