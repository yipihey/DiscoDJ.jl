# Does shell crossing leave an irreducible k² long-mode response? — paired-IC 1–7LPT vs collisionless PM

*Study of the "irreducible effective stress" claim of Carrasco, Hertzberg & Senatore (2012,
[arXiv:1206.2926](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2926)) with DiscoDJ.jl (DiscoDJNative, GPU).*

**Result in one paragraph.** Two 3D universes share their long modes bitwise and differ by a band of short modes
(0.2–1.2 h Mpc⁻¹); the long-mode displacement response Δψ_L = −C₂k²ψ_L is measured at the field level, with 1–7th-order
LPT (the pressureless single-stream fluid to increasing accuracy) and with a discreteness-suppressed collisionless PM
validated to reproduce 7LPT to 10⁻⁴ before shell crossing. **Before crossing** the single-stream series converges onto
PM (PM − 7LPT ≤ 2×10⁻⁵ against an 8 % 3LPT→7LPT change). **Immediately after first crossing** a coherent, pure-k²,
pressure-signed residual appears that is common to 3, 5, 7LPT and to the extrapolated series limit, while that series
is still convergent (0.225 < D < 0.5); it grows with the shell-crossed mass (∝ f^1.5), moves with the crossing time when
the band amplitude changes, is absent in redshift-space-only crossing, is resolution-converged, and is accounted for —
in sign and to a factor ≈ 2 in magnitude — by the velocity-dispersion stress measured directly in PM's multistream
regions. Varying the cutoff, the collisionless response runs like the CHS (2012) one-loop running while 4LPT/7LPT fail
once the integrated-out modes have crossed; it is carried by modes near the nonlinear scale, deeper modes decouple, and
it is a coherent effect (at k = 0.06 h Mpc⁻¹, modes below 5 h⁻¹Mpc change P(k) by −2.1 % coherently and add 0.05 %
stochastic power). **Assessment: strong support (category 1) for the CHS irreducibility statement within the window
where it can be tested (§12); beyond D ≈ 0.5 the single-stream series diverges and irreducibility is an
extrapolation.**

Study map: question & falsifiable predictions §0 · PreCrossedSheet PR #4 §1 · design & conventions §2 · methods §3 ·
convergence §4 · controls §5 · fiducial results (time × order, k⁴, stochastic, velocity) §6 · RG running & CHS

## 0. The question, and what would count as evidence

After coarse-graining, unresolved short-scale dynamics act on long modes through an effective stress
τ ⊃ c_s² δ_L, i.e. a long-mode displacement response

  Δψ_L(k) = −C₂(a) k² ψ_L(k) + O(k⁴) + ε(k).

The strong claim tested here: *the finite renormalized coefficient is an irreducible error of the
pressureless single-stream fluid* — making the perturbative pressureless solution more accurate does not
remove the part generated by multistreaming once shell crossing occurs.

We do not try to fit an EFT to a power spectrum. We construct two universes that share their long modes
**bitwise** and differ only by a band of short modes, evolve both with (i) the pressureless single-stream
solution to increasing order (1–7LPT) and (ii) the exact collisionless dynamics (PM N-body), and measure the
**field-level** response of the shared long modes. The hypothesis makes falsifiable predictions:

| | prediction if the hypothesis is right | what would falsify it |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | before any shell crossing, PM − LPT_n → 0 as n grows | a pre-crossing PM−LPT residual that does not converge (→ numerics or non-perturbative physics) |
| P2 | after crossing, PM − LPT_n tends to a nonzero limit as n grows, i.e. PM departs from the *limit* of the single-stream series | PM − LPT_n keeps decreasing with n after crossing |
| P3 | the departure has the EFT form −C₂k² (a k_max plateau), is deterministic (correlated with ψ_L) and grows with the shell-crossed mass | departure not ∝ k², or uncorrelated with ψ_L (stochastic), or unrelated to crossing |
| P4 | stochastic UV→IR leakage is suppressed at low k (P_ε,δ ∝ k⁴) and is separable from the deterministic part | |
| P5 (EFT) | the response is dominated by modes near the nonlinear scale; very deep UV modes decouple | response grows with the deepest UV modes |

## 2. Experimental design

**Background.** Einstein–de Sitter (Ω_m = 1, D₊ = a) with the Planck18 linear spectrum *shape*
(σ₈ = 0.8105 at D = 1). In EdS the general-order nLPT of `compute_core` (Ψ = Σ Dⁿψₙ) is the exact
fastest-growing single-stream solution, and PM integrates the same equations — so "finite LPT order" is the
*only* difference between the single-stream curves, and there is no growth-approximation confound between
LPT and PM. Time is labelled by D = a.

**Paired initial conditions** (`src/ics.jl`). One real-space unit white-noise field per seed on a fixed
256³ grid; potential φ(k) = ω̂(k)√(P(k)(N/L)³)(−1/k²) (DiscoDJNative faithful convention, ψ₁ = −∇φ):

  ω̂_IR = s_L·1[|k| < k_split] ω̂_L,   ω̂_UV = ω̂_IR + A·1[k_lo ≤ |k| < k_uv] ω̂_S.

Every mode with |k| < k_split is **bitwise identical** in the two members (tested), so there is no initial
k² difference — unlike Gaussian-filter pairs (§5). Fiducial: L = 500 h⁻¹Mpc, k_split = 0.2, k_uv = 1.2
h Mpc⁻¹ (band σ_δ = 1.91 D, σ_ψ = 4.0 D h⁻¹Mpc; long modes σ_δ = 0.71 D). The response is measured at
k ≤ 0.08 ≪ k_split (6 fundamental shells; 0.0126 ≤ k ≤ 0.19 stored). Realizations are embedded *exactly*
(mode by mode) into finer particle grids, so LPT on 256³ and PM on 384³/512³ evolve the same universe.

**Ensemble structure** (`config/*.toml`, `[seeds]`). For each long seed L and short seed S both long-mode
signs s_L = ±1 are run:

* the *odd-in-L* combination ½[Δψ(+L,S) − Δψ(−L,S)] removes the L-independent short×short term *exactly*
  — it isolates the deterministic response;
* the *even-in-L* part ½[Δψ(+L,S) + Δψ(−L,S)] carries the stochastic leakage;
* several short seeds at fixed L give the conditional mean ⟨Δψ|L⟩ and the conditional variance
  Var_{S|L}(Δψ) directly (Experiment B).

Errors are delete-one jackknife over long seeds (the independent unit).

**Observables** (`src/measure.jl`, all on the GPU, reduced to the low-k Fourier modes on the device):
Lagrangian displacement ψ(q) = x − q; velocity Π = dΨ/dD; CIC density (window-deconvolved); the
fraction of the 6N³ equal-mass Kuhn tetrahedra whose orientation is inverted (shell-crossed mass).

**Estimators** (`src/estimators.jl`, `scripts/analyze.jl`). With x = ψ_L∥ = D k̂·ψ̂₁ (the exact linear
displacement, identical in both members):

  R(k) = Σ Re(Δψ_∥ x*) / Σ|x|²  (binned),  R = −C₂k² [+ C₄k⁴] fitted for k ≤ k_max (12 cutoffs);

the same with the Eulerian density (regressor δ_L = −i|k|x) and with the velocity (regressor ψ₁∥). The
PM − LPT_n residual is computed **realization by realization** (Δψ_PM − Δψ_LPTn regressed on x), which cancels
the common short×short noise; the single-stream limit LPT_∞ is estimated from the odd partial sums
(3, 5, 7) by a Shanks/geometric-tail transform where the increment ratio is < 0.7.

**Dimensional dictionary.** C₂ is a comoving length². A stress term −c_s²(k/a)²δ in the EdS growth
equation, δ'' + (3/2a)δ' − (3/2a²)δ = −k² ℓ²(a) δ/a² with ℓ² ≡ c_s²/(aH)², has for ℓ² ∝ a^m the
particular solution Δψ/ψ_L = −k² ℓ²/[m(m+5/2)]. Hence

  ℓ²(a) = m(m + 5/2) C₂(a),  c_s²(a) = (aH)² ℓ²(a),  m = d ln C₂/d ln D,

valid only where C₂ is a power law. For the density, ΔP/P_L = 2R = −2C₂k² (the P_UV = P_IR − 2c_s²k²P_L
convention of the PreCrossedSheet Lab and of CHS up to their k_NL normalisation). We do **not** identify
our band-response C₂ with the CHS c_s² of the full ΛCDM field: it is the response to *one added band*, and
the mapping above assumes time-local stress.

## 3. Numerical methods

* **nLPT 1–7** — DiscoDJNative `compute_core` (general-order EdS recursion, longitudinal + transverse,
  3/2-rule de-aliased), Float32 on the GPU. Grid-converged: 256³ and 384³ give the same C₂ to < 0.5 % at
  every order and epoch (`config/conv_lpt384.toml`).
* **PM** — DiscoDJNative `run_nbody` (faithful port of DISCO-DJ, BullFrog stepper, 200 steps uniform in D
  from D = 0.02 with 3LPT ICs; every epoch is a step node).
* **Discreteness was the dominant numerical issue** (§4): a standard CIC/finite-difference PM does *not*
  reproduce the two-loop (D⁴) part of the pre-crossing response even at 512³ particles on a 1024³ mesh. The
  production PM therefore uses DISCO-DJ's discreteness-suppression settings (arXiv:2309.10865): PCS
  assignment, window deconvolution, spectral gradient, interlacing, and 2³ Fourier **sheet resampling**
  (the displacement field is Fourier-interpolated to 8 sub-particles per particle, i.e. the density is that
  of the continuous Lagrangian sheet). This reproduces 7LPT to ~10⁻⁴ before crossing and is converged
  after it (256³ vs 384³).

DiscoDJNative changes made for this study (commits on this branch): Float32 deconvolution fix (982cc10);
memory-lean PM solver `PMSolverLean` / `run_nbody(lean=true, callback_when=…)` (4e61176), which puts
512³ particles on a 1024³ mesh on the 48 GB GPU in Float32 at no accuracy cost.

## 4. Numerical convergence — what had to be fixed before any physics

**LPT.** 256³ and 384³ agree to < 0.5 % for every order and epoch (`conv_lpt256` vs `conv_lpt384`), including
orders 5–7 whose intermediate fields formally extend beyond the 256³ Nyquist frequency. The odd-order
series is the object of interest: in the ensemble mean only odd orders contribute to R (the even orders' cross
term with ψ_L is an odd-point function of a Gaussian field), and indeed C₂^(2n) ≈ C₂^(2n−1) (Table 1).

**PM: particle discreteness, not time stepping, was the problem** ([fig10](figures/fig10_pm_convergence.png)).
A standard PM (CIC, 4th-order finite-difference force) reproduces the *one-loop* (3LPT) pre-crossing response but
misses the two-loop D⁴ part, by an amount that depends on the particle grid:

| PM (one realization) | PM − 7LPT at D = 0.2 | at D = 0.4 |
|---|---|---|
| CIC 256³ / 512³ mesh | 0.0218 | 0.181 |
| CIC 384³ / 768³ | 0.0084 | 0.121 |
| CIC 384³ / 768³, 400 instead of 200 steps | 0.0084 | 0.121 |
| CIC 512³ / 1024³ (`PMSolverLean`) | 0.0042 | 0.1005 |
| Richardson limit of the CIC sequence (p ≈ 2.4–2.9) | ≈ 0.000 | ≈ 0.080 |
| **suppressed\*, 256³ / 512³ (production)** | **0.0001** | **0.0797** |
| suppressed\*, 384³ / 768³ | −0.0002 | 0.0803 |

\*PCS + deconvolution + spectral gradient + interlacing + 2³ Fourier sheet resampling (the DISCO-DJ
discreteness-suppression settings; the density is effectively that of the continuous Lagrangian sheet).
The band (k ≤ 1.2) reaches 0.75 of the 256³ particle Nyquist frequency, and its *nonlinear products* reach the
particle Nyquist, so a lattice of point masses cannot follow the fluid there; doubling the time steps changes
nothing (10⁻⁴). Two independent routes to the continuum collisionless answer — extrapolating plain PM in
resolution, and suppressing discreteness directly — agree, also after crossing (0.080 vs 0.0797 at D = 0.4). The
suppressed PM at 256³ and 384³ agree to ≲ 10 % of the residual after crossing and to 10⁻⁴ before. A naive PM
comparison would have reported a large spurious "pre-crossing EFT residual".

Other checks (`scripts/diagnostics.jl`, `results/diagnostics_fiducial.json`):
* PM total momentum |ΣP|/(N³ rms P) ≤ 1.8×10⁻⁴ over all runs and epochs (the spectral-gradient/interlaced force
  is not exactly momentum conserving; plain CIC/FD conserves it to round-off). Mass is conserved exactly.
* Isotropy: C₂ from modes near the grid axes (Σk̂ᵢ⁴ > 0.7) and near the diagonals (< 0.5) agree within 1–2σ up to
  D = 0.6 (e.g. −0.2172 ± 0.0018 vs −0.2169 ± 0.0019 at D = 0.3); at D = 1 they differ by 4.5 %
  (1.609 ± 0.015 vs 1.540 ± 0.013, 3.6σ) — a modest late-time grid anisotropy, larger than the 0.7 % axis/diagonal
  difference of 7LPT on the same modes.
* The stochastic residual of the density is ∝ k^4.0–4.2 and of the displacement ∝ k^2.0 (mass and momentum
  conservation).

## 5. Controls

* **Null pair** (band amplitude 0): Δψ ≡ 0 at every LPT order (test); the PM null pair measures the GPU
  reproducibility floor (`config/null.toml`).
* **Shared long modes** are bitwise identical (test), and **1LPT gives C₂ ≡ 0** at every epoch (Table 1).
* **IC artifacts** ([fig11](figures/fig11_ic_controls.png), `scripts/controls.jl`): a PreCrossedSheet-style
  Gaussian pair (R = 3 / 6 h⁻¹Mpc) has a 1LPT "response" C₂ = −14.0 (analytic −13.5 = (R_UV²−R_IR²)/2) at
  *all* D — ~80× the dynamical 3LPT response at D = 0.3; "match linear variance" adds a k-independent offset
  that a k² fit turns into C₂ = +73. A dynamical response must vanish as D → 0; these do not.
* **Odd/even in L**: with both signs of every long realization, the plain estimator *is* the odd estimator
  (x(−L) = −x(+L)), so the L-independent short × short term cancels exactly in R.
* **Estimator recovery**: injected −C₂k² + C₄k⁴ responses and k² stochastic noise are recovered (tests).
* **Kinematic check**: 1LPT density response = σ²_ψ,band D²/6 = 2.67 D² (measured 2.64–2.80 for D = 0.2–0.8),
  a pure projection effect with no dynamics.
* **Time stepping, mesh, particle number, IC order and start time** — §4 and `config/conv_*`.

**Experiment A — the single-stream series converges onto PM before crossing, and PM leaves it at crossing.**
([fig3](figures/fig3_c2_vs_time.png), [fig4](figures/fig4_lpt_convergence.png))
* One loop: C₂^(3) = −2.05 D² (Mpc/h)², i.e. ≈ −0.128 σ²_ψ,band — short modes *enhance* the long displacement
  (negative C₂). Higher odd orders add −3.7 D⁴ and −12.9 D⁶.
* Before first crossing (D ≤ 0.2) PM − LPT_n falls by ~an order of magnitude per odd order and PM − 7LPT is
  ≤ 2×10⁻⁵ — 10⁻⁵ of C₂ while 7LPT − 3LPT is 8 %: the pressureless series converges onto the collisionless
  answer (prediction P1).
* The residual switches on immediately after first crossing (inverted fraction 2×10⁻⁴ at D = 0.225) and is
  then common to 3, 5, 7LPT and to the extrapolated series limit LPT∞ (Shanks transform of the odd partial sums,
  where their increment ratio < 0.7): at D = 0.3 it is 0.004, at 0.35 0.02–0.04, at 0.4 ≈ 0.08–0.15. It does
  **not** decrease with order (prediction P2). The odd series itself stops converging only at D ≈ 0.5
  (increment ratio → 1, fig4 right), so for 0.225 < D < 0.5 the comparison is with a *convergent*
  single-stream series: the departure is not LPT truncation error.
* Sign: the departure is positive (C₂^PM > C₂^LPT): the collisionless response is *less* enhancing — the
  pressure-like sign of the CHS c_s². PM's C₂/D² bottoms out at −2.47 (D ≈ 0.35), crosses zero at D ≈ 0.78 and
  reaches +1.51 ± 0.02 at D = 1 (net *suppression* of the long displacement), while every LPT order runs away
  negative.

**Universality with the crossed mass** ([fig12](figures/fig12_band_variants.png); variants `amp07`, `kuv06`, 2 long seeds
each, sharing the fiducial IR members). Reducing the band amplitude to 0.7 delays first crossing to D ≈ 0.3 and the
departure with it; a narrower band (0.2–0.6) changes the response amplitude. Plotted against the inverted mass fraction of
the UV member, the fractional departure (C₂^PM − C₂^LPT)/|C₂^3LPT| of all three set-ups collapses onto one curve
(< 1 % at 10⁻³ crossed mass, ≈ 10 % at 2 %, ≈ 50–80 % at 7–8 %), i.e. the departure is a function of how much mass has
shell-crossed, not of D or of the band. (The variants' errors are mode-counting estimates from 2 seeds.)

**The departure has the EFT form** ([fig7](figures/fig7_pm_vs_lpt.png), [fig5](figures/fig5_kmax_stability.png)).
(R_PM − R_7LPT)/k² is flat in k up to 0.19 h Mpc⁻¹ at every post-crossing epoch (prediction P3): a pure
−ΔC₂k² term, with no k⁴ structure visible. The PM C₂ itself has a genuine k_max plateau (−0.089 ± 0.002 for
k_max = 0.03–0.18 at D = 0.2; 1.46–1.51 at D = 1), and adding C₄k⁴ changes it by ≤ 5 %.

**k⁴ convergence** (`scripts/c4_convergence.jl`, `results/c4_convergence.txt`; band-edge control
`chs/band_edge.py`). The absolute C₄ is noise-limited, not set by the proximity of the band edge: the exact one-loop
response to the band [0.2, 1.2) departs from pure k² by only 1.2 / 2.6 / 5.1 % at k = 0.08 / 0.12 / 0.18, which shifts
a fitted C₂ by < 0.5 % and contributes a smooth, computable C₄ ≈ +2.5 D² (Mpc/h)⁴ (density); for k_split = 0.33 and
0.5 the edge effect is 2.5 % and 1.4 % at k = 0.18. The apparent k_max dependence of the ensemble C₄
(−17.6 ± 9.5 at k_max = 0.12 vs −5.2 ± 1.1 at 0.18, D = 0.2) is statistical. Convergence is therefore judged on
realization-level differences with fixed weights, where the cosmic-variance noise cancels. Before crossing the k⁴
part of the odd LPT series converges (ΔC₄ of 5−3 / 7−5 LPT: −0.022 / −0.004 at D = 0.2, −0.107 / −0.038 at 0.3),
more slowly than C₂ (increment ratio 0.36 vs 0.32 at D = 0.3, 0.58 vs 0.57 at 0.4, → 1 near D = 0.5, as for C₂).
The suppressed PM matches 7LPT in C₄ before crossing (ΔC₄ = 0.000–0.002 ± 0.001), whereas plain-CIC 512³ PM is
off by −0.11 ± 0.017 — C₄ is a far more sensitive discreteness diagnostic than C₂. After crossing, the suppressed
PM at 256³ and 384³ agree in ΔC₄ within ~1σ at every epoch (D = 0.4: 0.23 / 0.26 ± 0.05; D = 0.6: 4.29 / 4.34 ± 0.5;
D = 1: 71.3 / 71.4 ± 7). The multistream residual's own k⁴ part is small (≈ 6 % of its k² part at k = 0.15,
D = 0.4) but from D ≈ 0.35 it is comparable to 7LPT's k⁴ truncation, so at this split it is not separable from LPT
truncation; the RG splits k_split ≥ 0.33 (k/k_split ≤ 0.55 for k ≤ 0.18) test this with the same seeds.

**Experiment B — deterministic vs stochastic** ([fig6](figures/fig6_deterministic_vs_stochastic.png)).
With 6 short seeds at fixed long modes the conditional variance Var_{S|L}(Δ) is measured directly. At D = 1,
k = 0.064 h Mpc⁻¹: coherent ΔP/P = 2R ≈ −1.6 %, stochastic P_ε/P_L ≈ 7×10⁻⁵ — the coherent response
dominates by ~200×; P_ε,δ ∝ k^4.0–4.2 and P_ε,ψ ∝ k^2.0 at every epoch (mass and momentum conservation),
before and after crossing (prediction P4). UV → IR coupling at these scales is a coherent modification of
existing long modes, not added noise.

**Density vs displacement** ([fig8](figures/fig8_density_vs_displacement.png)). The Eulerian density response
is dominated by the *kinematic* smearing of the long mode by the short displacements: 1LPT gives
C₂,δ = σ²_ψ,band D²/6 = 2.67 D² with no dynamics at all; one loop (3LPT) gives 1.68 D² at D = 0.2 (SPT UV
limit: (61/105)·2.67 = 1.55). PM's density coefficient stays 1.6–2.3 D² through crossing (it does not change
sign), while 7LPT's density coefficient runs away (45 D² at D = 1). P(k)-ratio and density-based fits are
therefore far less sensitive to the multistream physics than the Lagrangian displacement.

**Velocity** ([fig9](figures/fig9_velocity_response.png)): the Lagrangian velocity response obeys
C₂^v = d(D C₂^ψ)/dD to 1–2 % up to D = 0.4 (finite-difference limited later) — a consistency check, not new
information.

**Time dependence / effective sound speed.** The local slope m = d ln C₂,δ/d ln D of the PM density
coefficient is ≈ 2.2–2.4 for 0.3 ≤ D ≤ 0.6 and falls to 1.4 by D = 1; the displacement coefficient changes
sign. Under the time-local power-law mapping of §2, ℓ² = m(m+5/2)C₂ ≈ 10–12 C₂,δ in the plateau region, but
the drifting m means **no single time-independent c_s²** describes the response, and the CHS assumption
(their Eq. 59) that c²(a) follows the one-loop (P13) time dependence fails for the multistream part.

## 7. RG running: the cutoff experiment and CHS (2012) Fig. 3

### 7.1 What CHS's Λ is, and how it maps onto a sharp split
CHS smooth the fields with a Gaussian W_Λ(k) = exp(−k²/2Λ²) (their Eq. 14; real-space width 1/Λ, so Λ = 1/3 h Mpc⁻¹
↔ σ_x = 3 h⁻¹Mpc), and the loops use P·exp(−k²/Λ²) (Eq. 49). Their running (Eq. 62, k_ext = 0.01) is
c²(Λ) = c²(∞) + lim_{k→0}[P13(k,∞) − P13(k,Λ)] / (−2k² Γ P), with the counterterm Green's factor of their Eq. 57 /
App. C Eq. 83, R_ct = −c² k²/(9ℋ₀²f₀²). In the k_ext → 0 limit P13 → −(61/105)k²σ_v²P, so the running depends on Λ
**only through the removed velocity variance** Δσ_v² = (1/6π²)∫P(1 − e^{−q²/Λ²})dq. A sharp split k_s is therefore
equivalent to the Λ that removes the same σ_v²: Λ = 1/3 ↔ k_s = 0.21, Λ = 1/6 ↔ 0.115, **Λ = 0.2 (a 5 h⁻¹Mpc
Gaussian) ↔ k_s = 0.136** (`chs/chs_running.json`, nearly identical for their and our spectrum).

**Units.** Our density coefficient is C₂,δ = c²/(9ℋ₀²f₀²). In our EdS run (f = 1, a = 1), c²[10⁻⁶ c²] = C₂,δ[(h⁻¹Mpc)²]
exactly; in the CHS cosmology (Ω_m = 0.25, f₀ ≈ 0.48) the same c² is a 4.4× larger response. At fixed σ₈ the response
C₂ (a length²) is the nearly cosmology-independent quantity; the c² normalisation carries f₀². Both are shown
([figR1](figures/figR1_chs_fig3_comparison.png), [figR1 in c² units](figures/figR1_chs_fig3_comparison_c2units.png)).

### 7.2 Reconstruction of CHS Fig. 3 (`chs/chs_fig3.py`)
From CAMB (linear + halofit-original) in their cosmology and their App. C one-loop kernels (verified against
50-digit evaluation): c²(∞) matched at k_ren = 0.16 = **0.614 × 10⁻⁶ c²** (quoted: 6.2 × 10⁻⁷); the running
c²(0.1) − c²(1) = 0.79 × 10⁻⁶ c² vs 0.77 digitized (+2.5 %), and the k_ren = 0.10 curve agrees with the digitized one to
2–3 % over Λ = 0.1–1 (`chs/chs2012_fig3_digitized.json`, calibrated on the axis labels and their N-body points). The
k_ren = 0.18 curve (0.44 vs ≈ 0.53) is sensitive to their exact time dependence, since P22/P = 1.2 there.

* **Which modes carry it** ([figR4](figures/figR4_band_contribution.png)): per shell, PM exceeds one-loop PT by up to

* **PM − PT is not k_s-independent** (0.9 → 0.05 from k_s = 0.12 to 0.33): the IR member also contains nonlinear modes
  below k_s and needs its own counterterm, so the measurement is the difference of two universes' counterterms. Only
  for k_s → 0 does PM − PT approach a CHS-style "c²(∞)" of the UV universe: ≈ 0.9–1.0 (h⁻¹Mpc)², i.e. ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ c² in

  ΛCDM, n_s, our band ending at k = 1.2) this factor-of-3 bracket is all the absolute comparison supports; the
  *running* is the robust comparison.
* **(k_split, D) map** ([figR3](figures/figR3_c2_map.png)): the PM − 4LPT departure switches on at nearly the same D for
  every split (it is carried by the shared UV member's multistreaming); the IR members' own shell crossing (contours)
  marks where each IR universe acquires its own counterterm.

## 8. Counts in spheres

Every RG member records top-hat sphere contrasts at identical centres (32³ lattice, R = 20–150 h⁻¹Mpc), their
Laplacian, the spheres of the field low-passed at every split, and the exact linear spheres (`scripts/cis_analysis.jl`,
[figR6](figures/figR6_counts_in_spheres.png)). Decomposition at k_s = 0.2, PM, D = 1:

| R | Δσ²/σ² total | coherent (k < k_s) | stochastic (k < k_s) | band leakage (k ≥ k_s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | −1.30 % | −3.49 % | +0.42 % | +1.77 % |
| 40 | −0.69 % | −1.29 % | +0.07 % | +0.54 % |
| 80 | −0.29 % | −0.52 % | +0.03 % | +0.20 % |
| 150 | −0.18 % | −0.26 % | +0.013 % | +0.07 % |

* The sphere variance **decreases**: the coherent response dominates the stochastic one 8–20×, and the added band's own
  modes leaking through the top-hat window (a projection effect, not coupling) offset about half of it at R = 20.
* Per sphere the stochastic scatter of Δδ_R (≈ 6.5 % of σ_R at R = 20) exceeds the coherent shift (≈ 1.8 %), so the
  Δδ_R–∇²δ_R scatter plots have correlation r ≈ 0.25–0.3; the coherent part dominates σ²_R because it enters at first
  order (2b) and the stochastic part at second order.
* **Real-space EFT test**: Δδ_R = C₂,R ∇²δ_R holds with an R-independent slope (1.52–1.63 for R = 20–150 at k_s = 0.2),
  and the slope equals the Fourier response R(k) weighted by k⁴W²_TH(kR)P to ~1 % at every R (k_s = 0.15, 0.2). It is
  below the k → 0 coefficient (2.0) because ∇² of a sharp top-hat weights k ≈ k_s, where −R/k² is smaller (k⁴ and
  band-edge terms). (For k_s = 0.33 the weighted prediction lacks the stored modes 0.25 < k < 0.33.)
* **Mass-flux decomposition** ([figR7](figures/figR7_flux_decomposition.png), shared modes, R = 20): before crossing
  (D = 0.2) kinematic diffusion alone (1LPT) gives −0.196 %, gravitational infall brings 40 % back (3LPT/7LPT/PM:
  −0.118 %). After crossing PM (−3.6 % at D = 1) loses *less* than free diffusion (1LPT −4.5 %), while the single-stream
  solutions run away (3LPT −8.4 %, 7LPT −42 %): bound multistream structures trap their mass (capping the diffusive
  flux the single-stream solution keeps growing), and their velocity dispersion acts as a pressure on the long
  displacement (C₂,ψ > 0) — §9 measures that stress directly.

## 10. Experiment 2 — is the departure the multistream kinetic stress?

**Measurement** (`config/stress.toml`, `scripts/stress_analysis.jl`; 4 long seeds × ±L, PM and 7LPT). At every
snapshot each member deposits (NGP) mass, momentum and kinetic moments with u = dx/d ln a on 64³ and 128³ cells
(7.8 and 3.9 h⁻¹Mpc) and forms the velocity-dispersion stress with the smooth single-stream flow removed per cell
(least-squares linear velocity field: τ = n[Cov(u) − Cov(u,x)Cov(x)⁻¹Cov(x,u)]; this suppresses the finite-cell
"dispersion" of a smooth flow 30–125× in tests). Cells containing particles of an inverted Lagrangian tetrahedron
are flagged as multistream; flagged cells with an unflagged neighbour are "boundary" (the outermost caustic /
splashback shell at that resolution). The longitudinal long-mode stress response c²_τ(D) = Re⟨k̂k̂:Δτ̂ δ_L*⟩/(ρ̄⟨|δ_L|²⟩)
drives the long displacement through the EdS Green's function y'' + y'/2 − (3/2)y = c²_τ a (′ = d/d ln a,
C₂,σ = y/a), giving the response the stress implies.

**Validation.** Before crossing the stress response of PM and 7LPT agrees to 3–4 digits (0.3217 vs 0.3217 at D = 0.2,
7.8 Mpc/h cells): the residual coarse-graining artifacts are identical and cancel. After crossing 7LPT's coarse-grained
"stress" explodes (×140 PM's at D = 1) because its crossed streams fly apart ballistically — but LPT's equations of
motion contain no stress, so this is a diagnostic of unphysical streaming, not a force. The test is therefore whether
**PM's own multistream stress** accounts for PM's departure from the single-stream series.

**Result** ([fig13](figures/fig13_kinetic_stress.png), `results/stress_stress.h5`):

| D | C₂,σ from PM multistream stress (7.8 / 3.9 Mpc/h cells) | measured PM − 7LPT | inverted mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.275 | 0.0055 / 0.0021 | 0.0011 | 0.26 % |
| 0.30 | 0.0125 / 0.0050 | 0.0040 | 0.61 % |
| 0.35 | 0.041 / 0.0185 | 0.0225 | 2.0 % |
| 0.40 | 0.090 / 0.046 | 0.076 | 4.3 % |
| 0.45 | 0.161 / 0.089 | 0.192 | 7.2 % |

* The kinetic velocity-dispersion stress of PM's multistream regions, propagated through the long-mode Green's function,
  has the **sign and magnitude** of the measured departure (against 7LPT and against the extrapolated LPT∞) over the
  range where the single-stream series still converges (0.25 ≲ D ≲ 0.45), within the factor-2 bracket set by the
  coarse-graining scale; it vanishes identically before crossing.
* The coarse-graining dependence is physical: on finer cells part of the multistream motion is resolved as bulk flow
  (Reynolds stress) rather than dispersion. A complete accounting at fixed coarse-graining scale would add the resolved
  Reynolds and gravitational stresses, which were not measured; the data therefore establish the multistream kinetic
  stress as a *sufficient* explanation to a factor ~2, not an exact budget.
* Early after crossing essentially all of the stress lies in **boundary cells** (young multistream regions are thin
  sheets and filaments, every flagged cell borders single-stream flow); by D = 0.5 the interiors carry 40–50 % on
  7.8 Mpc/h cells. This answers the "sharp features vs volume" question of §6: the stress is localised at the
  caustic/splashback layers while those are thin, and becomes volume-filling as the multistream regions thicken — the
  coefficient is set by the integrated momentum flux, not by the k-space sharpness of the caustics (it is also
  resolution-converged, §4).

## 12. Interpretation — which explanation each measurement rules in or out

| candidate explanation of the long-mode UV response | what it predicts | what is measured | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| IC / filter artifact | C₂ ≠ 0 at D → 0, already in 1LPT | sharp split: 1LPT C₂ ≡ 0; Gaussian pairs give −14 (Mpc/h)² at every D (§5) | ruled out for this design; dominant for Gaussian-filter pairs |
| ordinary pre-crossing mode coupling | k² response ∝ D² (one loop), converging with LPT order | C₂^(3) = −2.05 D², odd series converging, PM = 7LPT to 10⁻⁵ | present, fully captured by single-stream LPT |
| finite-LPT truncation | PM − LPT_n shrinks with n | before crossing: ~10× per odd order; after: common residual for n = 3, 5, 7 and LPT∞ | explains nothing after crossing within the convergent window |
| numerical PM error | resolution/discreteness dependence | plain PM *does* miss the D⁴ term (discreteness); suppressed PM converged (256³ ↔ 384³, Richardson limit of plain PM agrees after crossing) | controlled (§4) |
| stochastic UV → IR leakage | uncorrelated with ψ_L, P_ε,δ ∝ k⁴ | P_ε,δ ∝ k^4.0–4.2, P_ε,ψ ∝ k^2.0; 200× below the coherent part at k ≈ 0.06; ±L design cancels it exactly in R | real but negligible for the coherent response |
| kinematic (displacement-smearing) projection | density-only, no dynamics, = σ²_ψ/6 | 1LPT density C₂ = 2.67 D² exactly; absent from the displacement response | explains most of the *density* response; not the multistream effect |
| multistream effective stress | departure starts at crossing, k², pressure sign, ∝ crossed mass, carried by the stress of multistream regions | all of these (§6, §9, §10) | supported |

## 13. Final scientific assessment

**Category 1 — strong support, within a stated window.** A converged, deterministic, pure-k² long-mode response
remains relative to increasingly accurate single-stream dynamics, and it correlates with — and is quantitatively
attributable to — multistream evolution:
1. *Convergence before crossing, not after.* PM − LPT_n falls ~10× per odd order before first crossing and becomes a
   common, order-independent residual after it, while the single-stream series itself still converges (D < 0.5).
2. *Tied to real-space shell crossing.* Onset coincides with the first inverted tetrahedra (D ≈ 0.225); the residual
   scales as (crossed mass)^1.5; reducing the band amplitude delays both crossing and departure, and the fractional
   departure of all band variants collapses onto one function of the crossed mass; redshift-space crossing
   (D ≈ 0.13), a pure projection, produces no departure.
3. *EFT form.* (R_PM − R_7LPT)/k² is flat to k = 0.19 h Mpc⁻¹; C₂ has a k_max plateau; the k⁴ remainder is ~6 % at
   k = 0.15 and resolution-converged; real-space spheres obey Δδ_R = C₂∇²δ_R with the predicted R-dependence.
4. *Physical origin.* PM's own multistream velocity-dispersion stress reproduces the departure's sign and magnitude to a
   factor ≈ 2 (coarse-graining dependent), living first in the caustic/splashback layers and then in the interiors.
5. *Running.* With the cutoff, PM runs like one-loop/CHS while 4LPT and 7LPT fail; the response is carried by modes
   near k_NL and deeper modes decouple.

**What the data do not establish.** (i) "Irreducible" is demonstrated relative to the 7th-order single-stream solution
and its extrapolated limit only for D ≲ 0.5; beyond, the series diverges and the statement is an extrapolation.
(ii) The stress budget is the kinetic part only, coarse-graining dependent by a factor ~2; resolved Reynolds and
gravitational stresses were not measured. (iii) Absolute agreement with the CHS coefficient is only established to a
factor ≈ 3 (EdS vs ΛCDM, band-limited UV member, c² vs response normalisation); the running shape is the robust
comparison. (iv) One box size (500 h⁻¹Mpc), one spectrum shape, EdS background.

**Unexpected findings worth following up.** The pressureless response is *negative* (short modes enhance the long
displacement) and the multistream contribution reverses it; in density, multistreaming *reduces* the diffusive mass
flux (trapping) while adding pressure, so density-based EFT fits see a much smaller effect than the displacement;
plain PM silently misses the two-loop response through particle discreteness, and C₄ is the most sensitive diagnostic
of that; LPT converges onto PM in redshift space through redshift-space crossing.
